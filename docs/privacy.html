Privacy policy

Sparrow Street, last updated 11 November 2026

Who we are

Sparrow Street makes original animated Bible stories and songs for young children, published on YouTube. Contact: hello@sparrowstreetstories.com.

What our posting tool does

We use a small tool of our own (the "Sparrow Street Robot" on Meta, and the "Sparrow Street Pin Scheduler" on Pinterest) to publish our own pictures and videos to our own Sparrow Street Facebook Page, Instagram account and Pinterest account on a schedule, and to hide spam or unkind comments on our own posts.

The tool is used only by Sparrow Street, for Sparrow Street's own accounts. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.

Our YouTube uploader

We use a private tool of our own, the "Sparrow Street Uploader", to upload and schedule our own videos on our own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it, we agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's use of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

The uploader only acts on the Sparrow Street channel. It doesn't read, collect or store information about viewers or other YouTube users. It keeps only the links of the videos we upload, on our own computer. Its sign-in is kept securely on that computer and is never shared. Access can be withdrawn at any time in Google's security settings.

What information it uses

  • Our own account details (our Page, Instagram and Pinterest account IDs), so it can publish to them.
  • The text of comments left on our own posts, only to decide whether to hide a comment that contains spam, links from unknown accounts or unkind words. Comments are never stored, sold, shared or used for anything else, and hidden comments are not deleted.

We do not collect, store or share personal information about anyone who views or comments on our posts. We don't use cookies or tracking on this website. We never collect information from children.

Gifts

If you choose to give a gift on our Support page, the payment is taken by Stripe on its own secure checkout page. Stripe receives your card details and email address to process the gift and send your receipt, under Stripe's privacy policy. We never see your card details. We receive your email address, the amount and the date, use them only for our records and to answer any question you send us about your gift, and never share them or add you to a mailing list.

Children

Our social media accounts are for parents, grandparents and teachers. Our posts never ask children to follow, like, comment or message.

Deleting your data

We don't keep personal data. If you have commented on one of our posts and would like the comment removed, please delete it in the app, or email hello@sparrowstreetstories.com with a link to the comment, and we'll remove it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
