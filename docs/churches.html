A twenty-minute session

One way to use an episode with a group of under-sevens.

  1. Watch

    Play the episode. It runs about 10–11 minutes.

  2. Sing it again

    Stand up and do the memory-verse song’s actions together.

  3. Talk

    Ask the three questions from the video’s description.

  4. Pray

    Finish with a short thank-you prayer, like Nana does.
