Meet the neighbours on Sparrow Street.

Ezra, Hana and Nana Wren find the Bible in everyday life.

Watch on YouTube

Who’s who

Ezra, 6
Bouncy and full of plans, built from boxes and tape. He’s learning to wait and think first.
Hana, 4
Quiet and kind, she notices tiny things. She’s learning to say “I can try!”
Nana Wren
Ezra’s grandma. She tells Bible stories with paper puppets in her garden Story Nook.
Pebble
Hana’s tortoise. He never says a word.

Our promises to parents

  • Every story stays close to the words of Scripture
  • Every story is built around one memory verse
  • Original songs, characters and art
  • Never asks children to click, buy or subscribe
  • Calm, never overstimulating: no flashing, no rushing, no shouting

How it’s made

Flat paper, on purpose
Simple cut-paper pictures and a gentle pace are easy on little eyes and ears. That’s why we don’t make busy, fast 3D cartoons.
Small and independent
Made in the UK, one episode at a time.
Kept free by gifts
Every episode is paid for by grown-ups who give.
Not a registered charity
So gifts can’t take Gift Aid.
